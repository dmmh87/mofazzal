Add or edit research topics

Changes here do not affect the live site until you upload the files to GitHub.

How it works
  1. Choose a topic on the left, or click Add new research topic. Fill in the text and add pictures from your computer.
  2. Click Download files. You get an updated content.js plus a web-ready copy of each new picture.
  3. In your GitHub repository, click Add file → Upload files, drag in all the downloaded files, and click Commit changes. The site updates in 1–2 minutes.
